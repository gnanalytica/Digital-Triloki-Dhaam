# Proposal — Digital platform for Mandir Triloki Dhaam Eindhoven

Prepared for the board of Stichting Senskaar Triloki Dhaam.
Draft. All figures in euros, excluding VAT unless stated. Hourly reference rate: €75.

## Phase 1 — Website basics (live by Saturday 10 October 2026)

### Deliverables

1. New bilingual website (Dutch and English) with a language switcher on every page.
2. Correct, single-source facts: opening hours, address, contact, service times.
3. Weekly services and the Sunday service explained for first-time visitors.
4. Annual programme as real pages and a calendar, not a picture: one permanent page per festival, a subscribable calendar feed, search-engine event markup.
5. The twelve ceremonies, each with its own page (content supplied by the pandit).
6. Mantras, aartis, scriptures and teachings, recovered from the legacy site (39,000 characters), edited and translated.
7. Courses page with dates.
8. Social loop: links from the site to the temple's accounts, shareable festival pages for posts and bio links.
9. Redirects for the 82 legacy pages, sitemap, `trilokidhaam.nl` kept.
10. Hosting, launch and handover documentation.
