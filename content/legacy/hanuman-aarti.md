# Hanuman Aarti

Shri Hanuman Ji Ki Aarti Aarti Keeje Hanuman Lalaa Ki, Dushta Dalan Raghunath Kala Ki. Jake Bal Se Giriwar Kanpe, Bhoot Pishach Nikat Nahi Jhanke. Lanka So Cot Samundr Se Khai, Jat Pawansut Bar Na Lai.

De Bida Raghunath Pathaye, Lank Prajjawali Siya Sudd Laye. Jagmag Jyoti Awadpur Raja, Ghanta Tal Pabhawaj Baja. Shakti Bann Laga Lakshman Ko, Laye Sanjeewan Lakshman Jiyaye Peth Patal Tori Yam Kare, Ahiravan Ki Bhuja Ukhare.

Aarti Kejee Jeisee Teisee, Dhruv Prahalad Vibhisan Jaisee. Sur Nar Muni Aarti Utare, Jai Jai Jai Kapiraj Ucharen. Bain Bhuja Se Asur Sanhare, Dahini Bhuja Sur Sant Ubare.

Lank Prajjvali Asur Sanhare, Raja Ram Ke Kaj Sanware. Anjani Putra Maha Baldayak, Dev Sant Ke Sada Shayak. Vidhwans Lank Kiya Raghurayee, Tulsidas Kapi Aarti Gayee.

Jo Hanuman Ji Ki Aarti Gavee, Basi Baikunth Bahar Nahi Aavee.
